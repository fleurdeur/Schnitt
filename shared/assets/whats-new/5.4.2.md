# Schnitt 5.4.2

## Alight Motion effects, in full
Every bundled Alight Motion effect now imports with its own controls and its own shader. Tiles wraps at all four edges, Directional Blur has a real Strength slider, and Oscillate exposes Frequency, Magnitude, Angle, and Wave as keyframeable properties.

## Exact properties and keyframes
Choice menus now store the real Alight Motion values, so effects such as Bump Map, Channel Map, Hex Tile, and Mirror keep the option you picked. Three-part controls such as Color Tune and Colorize now show X, Y, and Z sliders instead of dropping the third value.

## OpenGL ES rendering
Effect previews and exports continue to use one GPU pipeline. The OpenGL ES backend in Settings, Graphics renders the same shaders used by export, so a look that works in the editor also works in the saved video.

## Importing projects and links
Imported Alight Motion projects and shared links keep every effect property and keyframe, even when an effect is still marked as external. Project settings are no longer replaced by defaults during import.

## Under the hood
Shader coordinate uniforms were aligned with Alight Motion screen, layer, and bounds spaces. Image textures used by Bump Map, Grunge, and RGB Noise are embedded into the effect definition so previews and exports find them.

## Playback clock
The playback timestamp in the timeline now rolls over at sixty seconds and never shows a seconds value above 59.
