Perfect Screen

Perfect Screen isolates a subject from a green or blue screen and keeps the edge clean. It runs on the GPU, so the matte updates while you scrub.

Quick start

  1. Set Screen Color with the eyedropper, or turn on Auto Sample Edges to read the four corners of the layer.
  2. Raise Threshold until the background disappears, then add a little Softness.
  3. Use Black Clip and White Clip to force weak pixels fully transparent or fully opaque.
  4. Add Edge Feather for hair and motion, and Spill Suppression / Despill Amount to remove green bounce from skin and clothes.

Controls

Screen Color is the sampled key colour. Threshold is how different a pixel must be from that colour to stay. Softness widens the transition. Spill Suppression removes reflected screen colour; Despill Amount sets how strongly the key colour is neutralised. Invert Matte keeps the background instead of the subject.

Tip: put Perfect Screen before colour correction and glow effects, and key a layer with an opaque background rather than a pre-keyed clip.
