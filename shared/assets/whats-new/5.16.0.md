# Schnitt 5.16.0

This release fixes reversed exports, adds the Gold AI tools and effects, and brings two new character effects.

## Reversed export

- A reversed clip now keeps the exact source frame size whenever any H.264 encoder supports it.
- If the device truly cannot encode that size, the whole picture is scaled uniformly instead of keeping only the top-left corner.
- A dropped frame now stops the job with a clear error instead of silently cutting motion out of the reversed clip.

## AI Tools from Gold

- GM Anime X4 and GM Soul X4 now use the real GM neural models, tiled at 128 pixels and stitched into a 4x result.
- Twixtor is the frame-interpolation tool in the AI shelf.
- Remove dead frames is a new tool. It samples the clip, drops duplicate frames and reports how many were removed.
- The original AI tools, model choices and project data stay compatible.

## New effects

- Eye Blink draws a movable pair of eyes with adjustable spacing, size, tilt, blink speed, phase, lid colour and crease.
- Hair Flow bends hair along a root-to-tip guide with width, sway, speed, strand density, anchor and curl controls.
- Both effects are editable in the Effects Library and keep their keyframes.

## Gold effect pack

- More than two hundred Gold effects now load with their original controls and shaders, including GM Beauty, GM Color Link, GM Circle Mosaic and the GM 3D packs.
- Effects that already have a native Schnitt version are not listed twice.

## Preview

- The preview chooses one detail level when playback starts instead of changing resolution mid-play, so the picture no longer jumps while it plays.
- Paused preview and still frames stay at full resolution.

## Export and projects

- Existing projects, XML files, keyframes, presets and export quality are unchanged.
