# Schnitt 5.9.5

Import and motion-blur fixes.

## Motion Blur

- Motion Blur now keeps the current layer sharp and visible, then draws the moving trail behind it.
- The trail follows keyframed position, scale, rotation and motion effects. A stationary layer stays sharp instead of turning black or white.
- The old shutter accumulation no longer replaces the layer with a dark stack of samples.

## Mirror

- Mirror now exposes Blend Mode and Offset, with Blending Mode and Offset imported from Alight Motion.
- Alpha, angle, style and their keyframes stay editable.

## Imported keyframes and settings

- Converted effects now write their mapped values into the new effect instead of leaving the old property names in place.
- Animated properties keep every keyframe, easing and value through conversion.
- Animated rows that have no matching control yet are kept in the project instead of being deleted.
- Unknown effects are kept whole rather than being discarded.

## Still included

- Fade In and Fade Out work as direct layer opacity.
- Project opening remains fast, with one file read and a background version snapshot.
