# Schnitt 5.4.3

## Tiles repeats the layer correctly
Tiles now uses the layer's real screen position and size. The image repeats across all four edges, mirror alternates the copies, row offset and rotation stay aligned, and Crop changes the tile size without stretching the artwork.

## Directional Blur blurs in one direction
Directional Blur now uses the Alight Motion sampling path, with a real Strength slider and a linear blur along the chosen angle. Imported Strength values are clamped to the usable range instead of producing empty or black frames.

## Imported effects keep their keyframes
Projects and links imported from Alight Motion keep every effect property, every keyframe time, every value and every easing curve. Effects are no longer replaced by their defaults, and properties whose names differ slightly are matched to the correct controls.

## Under the hood
The layer coordinate helper now converts screen pixels into the layer space used by Tiles and the other Alight shaders. Imported Alight effects use their own definitions and controls instead of a fallback approximation.
