# Schnitt 5.16.1

This update puts the new video tools on the Home Tools page, with their own cards, screens and controls.

## Home Tools

- A new AI video section appears first on the Tools page.
- Twixtor, Dead frame remover, GM Anime upscale and GM Soul upscale each have their own card.
- Every card opens a full tool screen with a video picker, the app theme, progress, cancel, save and share.
- Twixtor has 2x, 3x and 4x extra-frame choices.
- GM Anime and GM Soul run the bundled GM neural models at four-times scale.
- Dead frame remover keeps the original timing and sound while skipping repeated frames.

## Editor

- The same tools remain available on a selected video layer in the editor.
- Eye Blink and Hair Flow stay editable in the Effects Library.

## Export and projects

- Existing projects, keyframes, presets and export quality are unchanged.
