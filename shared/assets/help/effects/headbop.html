Headbop

Bounces and sways the layer to a beat with a squash-and-stretch accent.

Properties

  • Speed — how fast the bounce cycles.
  • Bounce — vertical travel as a fraction of the layer height.
  • Tilt — maximum rotation in degrees.
  • Squash — squash-and-stretch amount.
  • Sway — sideways drift as a fraction of the layer width.
