# Schnitt 5.4.5

## Playback clock stays at hours, minutes and seconds
The playback clock is back to the three-field format: hours, minutes and seconds. The final field is seconds and never goes above 59, so it rolls from 00:00:59 to 00:01:00.

## Under the hood
The extra millisecond field from 5.4.4 has been removed.
