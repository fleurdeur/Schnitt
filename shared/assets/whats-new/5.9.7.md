# Schnitt 5.9.7

Background updates, sharing into Schnitt, linked drives and folders, a real eyedropper, and no timeline clutter from expressions.

## Updates in the background

- Start an update, then go do something else. The download continues in a foreground service with a progress notification.
- Press Hide to close the update dialog without stopping the download.
- When the APK is ready, the notification opens the install dialog. If the download stops, the notification says why.
- The dialog tells you whether you can leave the app or should keep it open when notifications are off.

## Share into Schnitt

- Share a video, picture or sound from another app. It is added on top of the open project.
- On the home screen choose New project, one of your projects, or Cancel.
- Shared files are copied so they keep working after the other app closes.
- Ogg and FLAC shares are accepted even when the sending app uses their application MIME type.

## Drives and folders

- Media and Audio now have Drive or folder. Pick a USB drive, SD card or any folder with the system picker.
- Its clips appear in Media and Audio under the folder name.
- Unplugged drives are skipped, duplicate files are hidden, and unlinked folders stop showing without breaking clips already in a project.

## Eyedropper

- Effect colour rows now have an eyedropper button that follows the app theme.
- Drag over the preview to move the picker. A magnified lens above your finger shows the exact pixels and the colour code.
- Lift your finger to apply that colour.

## Expressions

- Add an expression and it applies immediately.
- Generated values stay hidden from the timeline, so they never mix with your own keyframes.
- The expression source is kept on the property and still exports correctly.

## Imported links and groups

- Downloaded Alight links are read as seekable packages. The first pass no longer decompresses every video just to find the manifest.
- Repeated media inside a package is stored once, so imported projects are smaller without re-encoding.
- Group layers keep the complete imported frame, clip to it, and inherit the project size when their inner scene has no canvas.

## Preview and theme

- Added 1/8 and 1/16 preview quality for heavy projects.
- Resolution, frame-rate, duration and theme menus use the app theme colours.
- Projects saved by Schnitt skip the effect migration walk when no Alight effect is present; projects without links skip link resolution.

## Still included

- Motion Blur draws the real image or video.
- Import warnings list effects that could not be carried over.
- Settings > Advanced: debug log records imports, conversions, project opens and failures.
