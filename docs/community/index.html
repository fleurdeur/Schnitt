How it works

  1. Open the upload form and choose whether you are sharing a preset or an XML scene.
  2. Write the title and your GitHub name, then drag your file into the issue body.
  3. wall reviews the file. Adding the approved label publishes it here and in Schnitt's Presets screen.
  4. The community list in the app has two sections: Presets and XML.

Files must be non-destructive and free of personal data. Do not upload paid or copied presets you do not own.
