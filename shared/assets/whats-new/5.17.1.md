# Schnitt 5.17.1

## Smoother imported projects
Imported Alight Motion XML projects and share links now use a stricter performance profile. The preview measures its real render cost and lowers detail while playing instead of trying to draw every source frame.

Heavy imported scenes also render at a reduced size while paused, so moving, trimming and editing a stacked project no longer forces full resolution for every touch.

Playback is capped to a sustainable frame rate. A 120 fps import no longer asks a phone for twice the frames it can draw, and a struggling renderer drops to a stable 30 fps instead of stuttering through every frame.

## No more preview freezes while assets load
While the first media, fonts or effect shaders are still being prepared, the background preview now waits instead of looping through the timeline. This removes the long unresponsive period after opening a large imported project or link.

Video preview copies are also paused during playback, so making a proxy never competes with decoding the clip you are watching.

## Loading hints in the preview
The preview now says what it is waiting for:

- Loading project assets
- Preparing effects and shaders
- Preparing text and fonts

The hint disappears as soon as the required work finishes.

## Better effect cost estimates
The preview now counts hidden layers correctly, gives heavier effects such as blur, glow and particles a larger cost, and reduces quality further when a project stacks many effects on one layer.
