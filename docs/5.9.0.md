# Schnitt 5.9.0

## Perfect Screen, rebuilt as a virtual macro camera

Perfect Screen is now a complete screen-shoot simulator in one effect. It renders the LCD grid of a macro display, keeps its pixels stable when the camera is pushed against the glass, and adds real depth of field, glass bulge, chromatic fringing, dust specks, sheen and vignette.

The camera can be aimed by dragging the Target and Focus Point controls directly on the preview, or by using the new tilt, roll, zoom and focus sliders. Twenty presets cover static looks, reading sweeps, focus pulls and cinematic perspective moves.

## Movement without keyframes

Moving Target, Animate Angle and Moving Focus Point give continuous motion from three simple switches. Auto Zoom keeps the source layer covering the frame, Auto Exposure balances dark interfaces against bright screens, and Colour Tint adds warm, cool or green panel casts. Quality Scaling uses fewer samples during playback and automatically raises them for final renders.

## Faster depth maps

Depth maps now reuse unchanged frames, write their grayscale video in bulk, and divide CPU and XNNPACK work across worker threads without oversubscribing the phone. The result is unchanged, but long clips finish sooner.

## Daylight theme

Choosing Daylight in Select Your Theme now switches to the real bright Daylight palette. The label and the theme it applies match.

## After Effects presets

The Plugins screen now reads After Effects FFX preset files without running Adobe code. Effect names, parameter names and saved values are extracted and attached to the matching built-in effect as an editable preset. Existing JSX, JSXBIN, AEX and ZXP imports are unchanged.

## Other fixes

- Perfect Screen thumbnails and all twenty preset tiles are included.
- Motion Blur, Raster Extrude, Flex Layout and Animate Path Points keep working with the new theme and storage changes.
- Storage cleanup and the smaller preview cache from 5.8.0 remain in place.
