# Schnitt 5.9.2

Import fixes for projects and links made in Alight Motion, plus a real After Effects project reader.

## Alight Motion import

- Turbulent Displace and Turbulence keep their Stretch setting. Older single-value stretch is converted to the correct two-axis stretch at the current Scale, including keyframes and axis direction.
- Imported Motion Blur now renders the layer instead of a black or glitched frame. Sample bounds are clipped to the visible canvas and the blur uses the normal layer surface.
- Alight pivot points are folded into the imported position, so shapes, media, text and groups keep their original position, size and anchor when keyframes or scale are used.
- More Alight GLSL shaders import correctly: multi-target passes, sampler bias and gradient arguments, projected texture reads and nested buffer sampling are translated instead of dropped.
- General effect settings from imported XML are kept wherever a matching control exists.

## After Effects projects

- Binary After Effects projects now import compositions with their real layer order, source sizes, solid colours, media paths, in and out points, time stretch, parenting, visibility, position, anchor, scale, rotation and opacity.
- Transform keyframes are read from the project, including negative keys used by trimmed layers, and become normal editable keyframes.
- Text layers keep their text content instead of becoming plain rectangles, and precompositions become nested editable scenes.
- Audio-only items are no longer turned into visible layers.

## Optional Aurea bridge

- The ARM64 build also includes Aurea's native preset reader for ffx presets and jsx scripts. It is loaded only when that backend is requested and is never used for aep projects. The native project reader above remains the path for After Effects projects.

## Under the hood

- The project reader follows the published RIFX Egg! chunk layout and does not execute plug-ins or expressions. Effects and masks are not executed; geometry, sources and transforms are kept.
